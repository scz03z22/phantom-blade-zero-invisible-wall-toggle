# Changelog / 更新记录

## 0.1.0-beta

- F9 toggles the Pawn response on loaded `BP_SplineWall_C` dynamic-mesh components; the mod starts disabled.
- Scans for newly loaded matching walls approximately every three seconds while enabled.
- Restores responses recorded by the mod when toggled off.
- Prototype gameplay testing and mocked tests were performed; the packaged release still needs independent in-game testing.

---

- F9 切换已加载 `BP_SplineWall_C` 动态网格组件对玩家的碰撞响应，启动时默认关闭。
- 启用时约每三秒检查新加载的同类墙体。
- 关闭时恢复由模组记录并修改的碰撞响应。
- 已进行原型实机测试和模拟测试；发行包仍需独立实机复测。
